How much revenue are missed calls leaking from a small business each month? Answer three questions and find your number in seconds.

## What it does

An interactive, scripted simulation. The visitor enters three inputs — inbound calls per week, percentage of calls missed, average job value — and sees:

- Missed calls per month
- Revenue leaking per month and per year
- An illustrative recovery figure: what answering 9 out of 10 of those missed calls would be worth per month (clearly labeled as an estimate from the visitor's own inputs, not a guarantee)

The demo ends on a booking CTA to a free discovery call with Bilal Ali.

## Honest framing

This is a scripted simulation. No real calls are placed, no data is collected, and no client names, numbers, or results are used. All figures are illustrative estimates computed from the visitor's own inputs.
